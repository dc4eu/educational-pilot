# User Satisfaction Survey for Educational Institutions

This institutional survey is part of the European Large Scale Pilot DC4EU, under Work Package 5 (WP5), which focuses on education and professional qualifications.

It is aimed at gathering structured feedback from higher education institutions, VET providers and qualification authorities actively involved in deploying EUDI Wallet-based services. Several updates have been integrated into this version to reflect the governance, technical and legal requirements emerging from the pilot activities and DC4EU WP5 discussions.

These include alignment with eIDAS2, and the European Learning Model (ELM). The goal is to assess institutional readiness, training adequacy, data governance concerns and the practical interoperability of credential workflows with existing administrative and academic systems.

When you submit this form, it will not automatically collect your details like name and email address unless you provide it yourself.
