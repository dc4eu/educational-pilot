# End User Satisfaction Survey for Students

This survey is part of the European Large Scale Pilot DC4EU, specifically Work Package 5 (WP5), which focuses on education (all levels, formal and informal) and professional qualifications.

The aim is to monitor the actual experience of students and learners interacting with the EUDI Wallet during credential issuance and verification processes. Several enhancements have been introduced to this version of the survey based on stakeholder feedback, DC4EU WP5 discussions, and alignment with eIDAS2. The purpose is to better assess the usability, interoperability and real-life impact of verifiable credentials in learning and mobility contexts.

These changes help identify usability gaps, training needs, and interoperability issues while also enabling analysis of learner comprehension, trust, and agency regarding credentials and authorisation processes.

When you submit this form, it will not automatically collect your details like name and email address unless you provide it yourself.
