# Business Blue Print for Education and Professional Qualifications

## Introduction

The Business Blue Print for Education and Professional Qualifications (BBP) presents a comprehensive framework for digital credential management across the European Union. Developed under the DC4EU project, this blueprint establishes the foundation for trusted, interoperable, and privacy-preserving digital credentials that support educational mobility and professional recognition throughout Europe.

Building upon the European Digital Identity framework (eIDAS 2.0), the BBP addresses the fundamental challenges facing educational institutions, students, and professionals in managing and verifying qualifications across borders. It provides a structured approach to implementing digital credentials that respect institutional autonomy while ensuring cross-border recognition and trust.

## Blueprint Content

This blueprint serves multiple stakeholder groups within the European education and professional qualification ecosystem:

**Educational institutions** will find detailed guidance on implementing digital credential systems that maintain their autonomy while participating in a European-wide trust framework. The blueprint provides practical pathways for transitioning from traditional credential management to secure digital systems.

**Students and professionals** benefit from a system that puts them in control of their educational achievements. The framework ensures that learning truly belongs to the learner, enabling secure, portable, and verifiable credentials that support lifelong learning and career mobility.

**Policy makers and regulatory bodies** receive a comprehensive framework that aligns with European digital transformation goals while respecting national education sovereignty. The blueprint demonstrates how digital solutions can enhance rather than replace existing quality assurance mechanisms.

**Technology providers** can leverage the detailed technical specifications and data models to build interoperable solutions that serve the diverse needs of the European education sector.

The blueprint combines theoretical foundations with practical implementation guidance, supported by real-world use cases that demonstrate the framework's applicability across different educational and professional contexts. Through its comprehensive approach, it establishes the groundwork for a trusted digital credential ecosystem that serves all European citizens.
