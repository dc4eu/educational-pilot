# Annex A: Glossary of terms

## Introduction

This glossary provides comprehensive definitions of technical terms, acronyms, and concepts used throughout the Business Blueprint for Education and Professional Qualifications. The terms are organised alphabetically to facilitate quick reference and ensure consistent understanding across all stakeholder groups involved in the implementation of digital credential systems.

## Purpose and scope

The glossary serves multiple functions:
- **Standardisation**: Ensures consistent terminology usage across the framework
- **Clarity**: Provides clear, accessible definitions for technical and policy terms
- **Reference**: Acts as a quick reference guide for implementers and stakeholders
- **Alignment**: Supports alignment with European and international standards

## Term categories

| Category | Description | Examples |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Technical standards** | International and European technical specifications | W3C-VC, eIDAS, DID |
| **Educational frameworks** | European education and qualification systems | EQF, ESCO, EHEA |
| **Identity management** | Digital identity and credential technologies | PID, EducationalID, MyAcademicID |
| **Regulatory frameworks** | Legal and compliance requirements | GDPR, SDG, OOP |
| **Professional systems** | Sector-specific professional qualification tools | CPD, FMC, SEAFORMEC |

## A-C

### CPD (Continuous Professional Development)
**Definition**: Ongoing education for professionals to maintain and enhance skills in their fields, often a requirement for professional licensing and career progression.

**Context**: Essential component of professional qualification management systems described in [Chapter 7: Use cases and implementation scenarios](../chapter7.md#773-professional-qualifications).

**Related terms**: FMC, SEAFORMEC, Professional qualifications

---

### DID (Decentralised Identifier)
**Definition**: A globally unique persistent identifier that does not require a centralised registration authority and enables cryptographic verification of credential ownership and validity. DIDs are critical components of verifiable credentials, supporting secure, decentralised identity management.

**Technical specification**: Implements W3C DID standards for institutional identity management as detailed in [Chapter 8: Technical framework and sectorial EAA's catalogue](../chapter8.md#82-core-data-model-architecture).

**Related terms**: W3C-VC, Verifiable Credential, Trust Framework

---

### DLT (Distributed Ledger Technology)
**Definition**: A technology that uses a decentralised network to store data in a secure, tamper-proof format, supporting blockchain and other applications.

**Implementation context**: Provides the trust infrastructure foundation described in [Chapter 4: Operational model](../chapter4.md#41-trust-model-and-governance-framework).

**Related terms**: EBSI, Trust Framework, StatusList2021

---

## D-E

### EBSI (European Blockchain Services Infrastructure)
**Definition**: A European Union blockchain initiative providing secure, cross-border services including verifiable credentials for educational and professional credentials.

**Framework integration**: Supports the technical infrastructure requirements outlined in [Chapter 8: Technical framework and sectorial EAA's catalogue](../chapter8.md).

**Related terms**: DLT, eIDAS, Trust Framework

---

### EDCI (Europass Digital Credentials Infrastructure)
**Definition**: A framework developed by the European Commission that implements standardised digital credentials based on the European Learning Model and W3C Verifiable Credentials standards. It provides infrastructure for issuing, storing, and verifying digital credentials across Europe.

**Implementation**: Core component of the data models described in [Chapter 9: Data models](../chapter9.md#93-europass-digital-credentials-european-learning-model-v32).

**Related terms**: ELM, W3C-VCDM, Europass

---

### EDSSI (European Digital Student Service Infrastructure)
**Definition**: An infrastructure supporting student mobility within the EU, providing services for secure digital credentialing and verification.

**Mobility support**: Enables the cross-border use cases demonstrated in [Chapter 7: Use cases and implementation scenarios](../chapter7.md#771-non-foundational-identity).

**Related terms**: EHEA, MyAcademicID, Student mobility

---

### EducationalID
**Definition**: A unique digital identifier assigned to individuals within the education sector, used in digital credentialing systems to manage records securely and efficiently.

**Data model**: Comprehensive specification provided in [Chapter 9: Data models](../chapter9.md#91-educationalid).

**Technical implementation**: Follows the standards outlined in [Chapter 6: The education and professional qualifications sectorial rulebook](../chapter6.md#61-natural-persons-identity).

**Related terms**: MyAcademicID, Non-foundational Identity, PID

---

### EHEA (European Higher Education Area)
**Definition**: A collaborative network of European countries aiming to ensure comparability of higher education standards and quality to support student mobility.

**Policy context**: Fundamental to the European education landscape described in [Chapter 2: The European education and qualifications landscape](../chapter2.md#21-decentralised-authority-and-member-state-autonomy).

**Related terms**: Bologna Process, EQF, Student mobility

---

### eIDAS (Electronic Identification, Authentication and Trust Services Regulation)
**Definition**: Regulation (EU) No 910/2014, governing digital identification and trust services for electronic transactions within the EU, supporting interoperability and legal recognition of digital credentials.

**Regulatory framework**: Central to the compliance requirements detailed in [Annex D: Regulatory references](../annexes/AnnexD/README.md).

**Amendment**: Enhanced by eIDAS 2.0 (EU) 2024/1183 introducing Electronic Attestations of Attributes.

**Related terms**: EUDI Wallet, Electronic Attestation of Attributes, PID

---

### ELM (European Learning Model)
**Definition**: A data model based on W3C Verifiable Credentials, specifically designed for educational credentials in Europe. It defines the structure and relationships for representing educational achievements, qualifications, and learning outcomes in a standardised format.

**Technical specification**: Detailed implementation in [Chapter 9: Data models](../chapter9.md#93-europass-digital-credentials-european-learning-model-v32).

**Standards basis**: Built upon W3C Verifiable Credentials model for European education systems.

**Related terms**: W3C-VCDM, EDCI, Europass Digital Credentials

---

### ELMO (European Learner Mobility Ontology)
**Definition**: A data model used within the Erasmus Without Papers (EWP) network to facilitate cross-border student data exchanges.

**Interoperability**: Supports data exchange standards discussed in [Chapter 2: The European education and qualifications landscape](../chapter2.md#29-data-models-and-ontologies).

**Related terms**: EWP, Student mobility, Data interoperability

---

### EQF (European Qualifications Framework)
**Definition**: A reference framework to help compare qualifications across EU countries, supporting transparency and recognition of qualifications.

**Framework integration**: Essential component of the technical framework described in [Chapter 8: Technical framework and sectorial EAA's catalogue](../chapter8.md#823-alignment-with-european-frameworks).

**Policy reference**: Recommendation (2008/C 111/01) detailed in [Annex D: Regulatory references](../annexes/AnnexD/README.md).

**Related terms**: ESCO, Qualification recognition, Learning outcomes

---

### ESCO (European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations)
**Definition**: A multilingual classification system by the European Commission that identifies and categorises skills, competencies, qualifications, and occupations relevant for the EU labour market and education/training. It serves as a common reference terminology for digital credentials.

**Technical integration**: Core component of the standards alignment described in [Chapter 8: Technical framework and sectorial EAA's catalogue](../chapter8.md#823-alignment-with-european-frameworks).

**Skills mapping**: Enables the skill-based recognition capabilities outlined in [Chapter 6: The education and professional qualifications sectorial rulebook](../chapter6.md#67-education-and-professional-qualifications-ontology---european-learning-model-elm).

**Related terms**: EQF, Skills recognition, Labour mobility

---

## F-M

### FMC (Further Medical Competence)
**Definition**: Advanced qualifications for healthcare professionals to demonstrate specific competencies and advanced skills.

**Professional context**: Integral to healthcare professional qualification use cases in [Chapter 7: Use cases and implementation scenarios](../chapter7.md#773-professional-qualifications).

**Related terms**: CPD, SEAFORMEC, UEMS

---

### GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
**Definition**: Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the EU's legal framework for data protection and privacy, critical for protecting personal data in digital credentialing.

**Privacy framework**: Fundamental to the privacy protection measures described in [Chapter 6: The education and professional qualifications sectorial rulebook](../chapter6.md#612-enforcement-policy-agent).

**Compliance requirements**: Detailed in [Annex D: Regulatory references](../annexes/AnnexD/README.md).

**Related terms**: Privacy by design, Selective disclosure, Data minimisation

---

### GUNet (Greek Universities Network)
**Definition**: A platform supporting eDiplomas and academic credential sharing, aiding in credential verification for Greek universities.

**Implementation example**: Demonstrates national-level credential systems described in [Chapter 2: The European education and qualifications landscape](../chapter2.md#27-electronic-diploma-issuance).

**Related terms**: eDiplomas, National systems, Digital credentials

---

### ISCED (International Standard Classification of Education)
**Definition**: A UNESCO framework that standardises education classification globally, supporting data comparisons and interoperability in education statistics.

**Classification standard**: Supports the technical framework requirements in [Chapter 8: Technical framework and sectorial EAA's catalogue](../chapter8.md#84-country-specific-implementations).

**Related terms**: Education classification, Data interoperability, Statistical frameworks

---

### MyAcademicID
**Definition**: An initiative providing students with a digital identity to support participation in the European Higher Education Area, aiding in secure and seamless access to cross-border educational services.

**Data model**: Comprehensive specification in [Chapter 9: Data models](../chapter9.md#92-myacademicid).

**Mobility support**: Enables the academic mobility use cases described in [Chapter 7: Use cases and implementation scenarios](../chapter7.md#771-non-foundational-identity).

**Related terms**: EducationalID, EHEA, Student mobility

---

## N-P

### Non-foundational Identity
**Definition**: An identifier used within specific sectors, such as education, distinct from foundational (e.g., national ID) systems, supporting identity verification while respecting sector-specific needs.

**Identity framework**: Core concept in the identity management approach detailed in [Chapter 6: The education and professional qualifications sectorial rulebook](../chapter6.md#61-natural-persons-identity).

**Implementation**: Demonstrated through use cases in [Chapter 7: Use cases and implementation scenarios](../chapter7.md#771-non-foundational-identity).

**Related terms**: EducationalID, MyAcademicID, Sectoral identity

---

### OOP (Once-Only Principle)
**Definition**: A principle in the EU's digital strategy ensuring that data collected from users is reused across services to prevent repetitive data entry.

**Policy framework**: Supports the efficiency objectives outlined in [Chapter 1: Introduction](../chapter1.md#14-connecting-with-europes-digital-future).

**Regulatory basis**: Single Digital Gateway Regulation (EU) 2018/1724 detailed in [Annex D: Regulatory references](../annexes/AnnexD/README.md).

**Related terms**: SDG, Digital efficiency, Data reuse

---

### PID (Personal Identification)
**Definition**: Personal data stored securely in digital credentialing systems, supporting identity verification within trusted frameworks.

**Identity verification**: Central to the natural person identity framework in [Chapter 6: The education and professional qualifications sectorial rulebook](../chapter6.md#61-natural-persons-identity).

**Technical implementation**: Enables the secure identity verification described in [Chapter 5: Natural persons and legal entities onboarding process](../chapter5.md#51-educational-onboarding-process).

**Related terms**: eIDAS, EUDI Wallet, Identity verification

---

### ProfessionalID
**Definition**: A unique digital identifier for managing records and credentials within professional fields, enhancing security and trust in digital credentialing.

**Professional credentials**: Supports the professional qualification management described in [Chapter 7: Use cases and implementation scenarios](../chapter7.md#773-professional-qualifications).

**Related terms**: Professional qualifications, CPD, Sectoral identity

---

## Q-S

### QR (Quick Response code)
**Definition**: A matrix barcode used for easy, secure access to digital information, commonly used in credentialing to validate and share digital documents.

**User interface**: Facilitates credential sharing mechanisms described in [Chapter 6: The education and professional qualifications sectorial rulebook](../chapter6.md#610-sharing-mechanisms).

**Related terms**: Digital sharing, Credential presentation, Mobile access

---

### SDG (Single Digital Gateway)
**Definition**: Regulation (EU) 2018/1724, facilitating online access to information and public services across EU Member States, supporting digitalisation and streamlined administrative processes.

**Digital services**: Enables the cross-border service objectives described in [Chapter 1: Introduction](../chapter1.md#14-connecting-with-europes-digital-future).

**Regulatory framework**: Detailed in [Annex D: Regulatory references](../annexes/AnnexD/README.md).

**Related terms**: OOP, Cross-border services, Digital transformation

---

### SEAFORMEC (Foundation for Medical Education and Training Accreditation)
**Definition**: An accrediting organisation for healthcare-related Continuous Professional Development (CPD) and Further Medical Competence (FMC) activities within Europe.

**Professional accreditation**: Central to the healthcare professional use cases in [Chapter 7: Use cases and implementation scenarios](../chapter7.md#773-professional-qualifications).

**Quality assurance**: Supports professional quality frameworks described in [Chapter 4: Operational model](../chapter4.md).

**Related terms**: CPD, FMC, UEMS, Medical education

---

### Selective Disclosure
**Definition**: A privacy-enhancing feature of verifiable credentials that allows credential holders to share only specific parts of their credentials while maintaining verifiability. This enables individuals to control their personal data and share only what is necessary for a specific purpose.

**Privacy technology**: Core privacy feature detailed in [Chapter 6: The education and professional qualifications sectorial rulebook](../chapter6.md#69-selective-disclosure).

**Technical implementation**: Supports GDPR compliance through privacy-preserving credential sharing.

**Related terms**: Privacy by design, GDPR, Data minimisation

---

### StatusList2021
**Definition**: A service for managing and verifying the status of digital credentials, supporting revocation and suspension functions to enhance trust in digital credentialing.

**Credential lifecycle**: Supports the lifecycle management described in [Chapter 4: Operational model](../chapter4.md#42-credential-lifecycle-management).

**Technical standard**: W3C standard for credential status management.

**Related terms**: Credential revocation, Trust management, W3C standards

---

## T-W

### Trust Framework
**Definition**: A set of rules, protocols, and governance structures that define how digital credentials are managed, issued, and verified within a system, ensuring standardisation and security.

**Governance model**: Comprehensive framework detailed in [Chapter 4: Operational model](../chapter4.md#41-trust-model-and-governance-framework).

**Implementation**: Technical specifications provided in [Chapter 8: Technical framework and sectorial EAA's catalogue](../chapter8.md).

**Related terms**: Governance, Security protocols, Standardisation

---

### UEMS (European Union of Medical Specialists)
**Definition**: A professional organisation collaborating with SEAFORMEC to accredit medical education, supporting standardised and recognised CPD for medical specialists across Europe.

**Medical specialisation**: Supports professional healthcare qualification processes described in [Chapter 7: Use cases and implementation scenarios](../chapter7.md#773-professional-qualifications).

**Related terms**: SEAFORMEC, CPD, Medical specialisation

---

### Verifiable Credential (VC)
**Definition**: A digital document issued by a trusted entity that can be cryptographically verified to confirm authenticity and enhance portability across digital systems.

**Technical foundation**: Core technology underlying all data models described in [Chapter 9: Data models](../chapter9.md).

**Standards compliance**: Implements W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model standards.

**Related terms**: W3C-VC, DID, Digital credentials

---

### W3C (World Wide Web Consortium)
**Definition**: The main international standards organisation for the World Wide Web, responsible for developing protocols and guidelines that ensure the long-term growth and standardisation of web technologies, including specifications for verifiable credentials.

**Standards authority**: Provides the technical standards foundation described in [Chapter 8: Technical framework and sectorial EAA's catalogue](../chapter8.md#823-standards-and-specifications).

**Related terms**: W3C-VC, W3C-VCDM, Technical standards

---

### W3C-VC (World Wide Web Consortium - Verifiable Credentials)
**Definition**: A standard developed by the W3C for creating, issuing, and verifying digital credentials. It enables trusted digital representations of credentials that can be securely shared across systems.

**Technical standard**: Foundation for the technical framework in [Chapter 8: Technical framework and sectorial EAA's catalogue](../chapter8.md#821-core-standards).

**Related terms**: W3C-VCDM, Verifiable Credential, DID

---

### W3C-VCDM (W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model)
**Definition**: The data model created by W3C to structure verifiable credentials, supporting interoperability and enabling secure, standardised credential issuance and verification.

**Data structure**: Fundamental to all data models specified in [Chapter 9: Data models](../chapter9.md).

**Technical specification**: Core component of the technical architecture described in [Chapter 8: Technical framework and sectorial EAA's catalogue](../chapter8.md).

**Related terms**: W3C-VC, ELM, Data interoperability

---
