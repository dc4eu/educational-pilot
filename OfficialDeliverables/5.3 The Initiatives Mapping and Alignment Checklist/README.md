## 1 Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose and Scope of the Document

The "Initiatives Mapping and Alignment Checklist" (IMAC) document for Education and Professional Qualifications serves as a comprehensive guide and reference point for the implementation of digital credentials within the European Union's education and professional qualification framework. It consolidates two key components: the Education and Professional Qualifications Rulebook and the Education and Professional Qualifications Case Study, providing a unified perspective on the operational, legal, and technical aspects of the DC4EU project.

The purpose of this document is to:

- Offer a structured overview of the governance, legal frameworks, and technical trust models underpinning the issuance and verification of digital educational and professional credentials.

- Highlight the practical insights and strategic recommendations derived from real-world piloting efforts in educational institutions and professional bodies.

- Serve as an onboarding resource for stakeholders, including Member States, educational institutions, professional bodies, and private entities, aiming to integrate with the DC4EU digital trust framework for education and professional qualifications.

- Provide guidance for implementing cross-border recognition of qualifications whilst respecting institutional autonomy and national educational sovereignty.

By combining theoretical frameworks with practical case studies, the IMAC document ensures a holistic understanding of the challenges and opportunities in implementing interoperable digital educational services across Europe, supporting both student mobility and professional recognition.

### 1.2 Structure of the Document

The IMAC document for Education and Professional Qualifications is organised into the following sections:

- **Education and Professional Qualifications Rulebook**: This section outlines the governance structures, legal and regulatory frameworks, and service descriptions necessary for the successful implementation of digital educational credentials. It emphasises semantic consistency through the European Learning Model (ELM), selective disclosure capabilities, and localised validation policies to ensure compliance with EU regulations such as eIDAS, whilst supporting European educational frameworks including the European Qualifications Framework (EQF) and the European Higher Education Area (EHEA).

- **Education and Professional Qualifications Case Study**: This section provides operational insights and policy recommendations based on the DC4EU pilot implementations. It showcases the application of Classical PKI and EBSI trust models in high-impact educational use cases, such as the issuance of digital diplomas (EUHED), educational identity credentials, and professional qualification attestations. The case study demonstrates privacy-preserving credential verification processes and cross-border educational mobility scenarios.

- **[Conclusion](./Conclusions/)**: A summary of key findings and next steps for scaling the DC4EU framework across Member States' educational systems, ensuring seamless integration with existing educational infrastructures whilst maintaining institutional independence and academic freedom.

## Key Characteristics of the Education and Professional Qualifications Framework

This IMAC document addresses the unique requirements of the education sector, including:

### Educational Mobility and Recognition
- Support for the European Higher Education Area (EHEA) objectives
- Integration with existing mobility programmes (Erasmus+, Erasmus Without Paper)
- Automated qualification recognition through EQF level mapping
- Cross-border credit transfer and accumulation systems

### Institutional Autonomy
- Respect for national education system sovereignty
- Preservation of institutional academic independence
- Flexible implementation supporting diverse educational traditions
- Accommodation of varying institutional technical capabilities

### Privacy and Data Protection
- Implementation of privacy-by-design principles for student data
- Selective disclosure mechanisms protecting sensitive academic information
- Compliance with GDPR requirements specific to educational contexts
- User-centric approach to credential sharing and verification

### Technical Interoperability
- Support for multiple trust models (Classical PKI, EBSI)
- Integration with European Learning Model (ELM) v3.2 specifications
- Compatibility with existing educational technology infrastructures
- Future-proof technical foundations supporting emerging educational innovations

### Quality Assurance Integration
- Incorporation of accreditation and quality assurance metadata
- Support for transparent quality frameworks
- Integration with national quality assurance agencies
- Maintenance of educational standards across borders
