# Governance Models in Education and Professional Qualifications

This section provides comprehensive documentation of governance frameworks in education and professional qualifications, examining both the European-level coordination mechanisms and their practical implementation within the DC4EU Large Scale Pilot.

The documentation is organised to guide readers from understanding the overarching European governance landscape through to the specific technical implementations that enable digital trust and cross-border recognition.

---

## Documentation Structure

### 1. [European Governance Framework](european-governance.md)
Comprehensive overview of how education and professional qualifications are governed across the European Union, examining multi-level governance structures from European Commission to Member States and regions. This document establishes the foundational understanding of governance principles, coordination mechanisms, and the balance between national competence and European frameworks.

### 2. [EAA-based Authorisation Model](eaa-based-authorisation-model.md)
Technical framework defining the Electronic Attestation of Attributes (EAA) model developed to digitally represent and verify governance structures. This document explains the authorisation model architecture, verification processes, and implementation approach that translates complex governance relationships into verifiable digital credentials.

### 3. [DC4EU Governance Implementation](dc4eu-governance-implementation.md)
Detailed explanation of how the different governance models have been practically implemented within the DC4EU project. This document demonstrates how European governance frameworks have been translated into operational digital trust systems with specific authorisations, country implementations, and institutional arrangements.

## How to Navigate This Documentation

**Start with European Governance Framework** if you need to understand the policy and regulatory context that shapes education and professional qualifications across Europe.

**Review the EAA-based Authorisation Model** to understand the technical approach and architecture used to implement governance digitally.

**Examine DC4EU Governance Implementation** to see practical examples of how these frameworks operate in real-world scenarios across participating Member States.

## Key Themes

**Multi-level Governance**: Documentation demonstrates how governance operates across European, national, regional, and institutional levels whilst maintaining coherent trust relationships.

**Digital Transformation**: Shows how traditional governance structures can be enhanced through digital credentials and verification mechanisms without disrupting established authority relationships.

**Cross-border Recognition**: Explains how governance frameworks enable mobility and recognition whilst respecting national competences and institutional autonomy.

**Practical Implementation**: Provides concrete examples of how abstract governance principles translate into operational systems supporting learners, professionals, and institutions.
