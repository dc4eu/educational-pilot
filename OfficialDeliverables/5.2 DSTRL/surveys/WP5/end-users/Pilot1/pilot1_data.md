## Summary Analysis for Official Pilot 1 PAs

### Response Coverage Issues
- **Only 2 out of 5** official Pilot 1 PAs provided end-user survey responses
- **Missing critical feedback** from OPH (Finland), DTU (Denmark), and Ladok (Sweden)
- **60% non-response rate** among official participants indicates potential survey distribution or engagement issues

### Comparative Performance

| Metric | AUAS | Sikt | Overall |
|--------|------|------|---------|
| **Recommendation Score** | 6.3/10 | 7.2/10 | 6.5/10 |
| **Problem Rate** | 61% | 33% | 57% |
| **Trust Level** | Moderate | Higher | Moderate |
| **Installation Success** | 71% easy/minimal issues | 83% easy/minimal issues | 73% |

### Key Findings

#### Strengths
1. **Sikt demonstrates better performance** across most metrics than AUAS
2. **Moderate overall trust levels** with 47-67% rather trusting the tool
3. **Installation generally successful** with 73% having easy or minimal issues

#### Critical Issues
1. **Feature satisfaction significantly below importance** across all features
2. **Language support critical gap** - especially for Norwegian users at Sikt
3. **In-app help and guidance severely inadequate** (1.5-2.7/5 satisfaction)
4. **Credential explanation and verification process clarity major weaknesses**

#### Priority Improvements
1. **Enhance language support** for Dutch and Norwegian
2. **Improve in-app help and guidance systems**
3. **Better credential purpose explanation** and verification process clarity
4. **Address technical stability issues** (crashes, slow performance)
5. **Strengthen integration** with existing institutional systems

### Data Limitations
This analysis represents only **44 responses from 2 institutions** out of the 5 official Pilot 1 PAs, providing limited insight into the full Pilot 1 user experience. The missing responses from OPH, DTU, and Ladok represent a significant gap in understanding Pilot 1 performance across all participating countries and institutional contexts.
