# DC4EU Piloting Agents Survey Analysis

**WP5 Survey Component - Institutional Feedback Collection**

---

## Survey Context and Purpose

The DC4EU piloting agents survey was designed to capture comprehensive institutional feedback from educational organisations implementing digital credential solutions across the three pilot implementations. This survey represents a critical component of Work Package 5's systematic user experience monitoring, focusing specifically on the institutional perspective rather than individual end-user experiences.

### Survey Overview
The survey targeted educational institutions, VET providers, and qualification authorities that served as official piloting agents within the DC4EU project. The primary objective was to assess organisational readiness, technical implementation experiences, and strategic positioning for post-project continuation across diverse educational contexts.

**Key survey characteristics:**
- **Participants:** 23 educational institutions across 9 European countries
- **Implementation focus:** Three distinct pilot approaches (classical PKI, decentralised PKI, and combined implementation)
- **Assessment scope:** Technical performance, organisational readiness, trust evaluation, and sustainability planning

## Survey Methodology and Analysis Framework

The piloting agents survey follows a standardised methodological framework that combines quantitative metrics with qualitative feedback mechanisms. The survey design emphasises systematic data collection across diverse educational contexts to enable evidence-based analysis of institutional readiness and implementation effectiveness.

### Key Assessment Areas
- **Organisational readiness** and technical implementation experience
- **Strategic positioning** for post-project continuation and sustainability  
- **Resource requirements** and institutional capability assessment
- **Trust evaluation** and privacy considerations
- **Training adequacy** and documentation effectiveness
- **Feature importance vs satisfaction** gap analysis

### Analysis Approach
Each pilot-specific analysis document provides:
- Participation overview and institutional response coverage
- Net promoter score analysis and recommendation likelihood assessment  
- Technical challenge identification and problem occurrence rates
- Trust and privacy confidence evaluation
- Training effectiveness and documentation quality assessment
- Key findings and targeted recommendations for improvement

For detailed survey methodology information, refer to **[Survey Methodology](../survey_methodology.md)** in the parent directory.
