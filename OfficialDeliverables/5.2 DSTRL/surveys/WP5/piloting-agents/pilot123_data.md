# DC4EU Piloting Agents Survey - Complete Responses by Pilot and Question

This document provides a comprehensive breakdown of all survey responses from educational institutions participating in the DC4EU project, organised by pilot implementation and individual questions to avoid any misinterpretations.

- **Pilot 1 (Classical PKI):** 2 responses from 2 institutions
- **Pilot 2 (Decentralised PKI):** 18 responses from 15 institutions  
- **Pilot 3 (Combined Implementation):** 6 responses from 3 institutions

## SUMMARY ANALYSIS BY PILOT

### Pilot 1 (Classical PKI) Summary
- **Institutions:** 2 (Sweden: 1, Netherlands: 1)
- **Average Recommendation:** 8.5/10
- **Problem Rate:** 50% (1 of 2 responses)
- **Key Strengths:** High trust levels, good training provision
- **Key Challenges:** Integration issues, lifecycle management, revocation functionality

### Pilot 2 (Decentralised PKI) Summary  
- **Institutions:** 15 across 6 countries (Sweden, Spain, Poland, Romania, Hungary, Portugal)
- **Average Recommendation:** 7.9/10
- **Problem Rate:** 56% (10 of 18 responses)
- **Key Strengths:** Generally good user interface satisfaction, broad institutional participation
- **Key Challenges:** Initial setup complexity, integration with existing systems, documentation gaps

### Pilot 3 (Combined Implementation) Summary
- **Institutions:** 3 (Netherlands: 2, Portugal: 1)
- **Average Recommendation:** 6.3/10
- **Problem Rate:** 67% (4 of 6 responses)
- **Key Strengths:** COFAC shows excellent results (9/10, no problems, complete trust)
- **Key Challenges:** High technical complexity at Twente and Saxion, integration difficulties
