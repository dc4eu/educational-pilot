# Work Package 9 (WP9) Survey Analysis

**Strategic Consultation for Post-Project Sustainability and eIDAS2 Preparation**

---

## WP9 Survey Context and Purpose

Work Package 9 conducted a comprehensive strategic consultation survey during the DC4EU Warsaw workshop to assess institutional readiness for the transition beyond the formal project conclusion. This survey represents a critical evaluation of production readiness, institutional dynamics, post-project commitment, and strategic vision for the future European digital credential ecosystem.

### Survey Context
The WP9 survey was specifically designed to capture honest assessments from piloting agents about the transition from the DC4EU project (ending 31st July 2025) to sustainable, community-driven continuation of digital credential initiatives. The survey addressed the practical realities of production deployment, eIDAS2 compliance preparation, and institutional commitment to ongoing digital credential adoption.

**Survey characteristics:**
- **Strategic focus:** Post-project sustainability and production readiness assessment
- **Real-time data collection:** Interactive workshop format using menti.com platform
- **Honest evaluation approach:** Designed to capture genuine institutional perspectives rather than aspirational responses
- **Future-oriented analysis:** Assessment of long-term commitment and strategic vision

---

## Survey Implementation and Methodology

### DC4EU Warsaw Workshop Consultation
The survey was conducted during the DC4EU Warsaw workshop on 27 June 2025, using a guided presentation format with real-time response collection. This interactive approach ensured high engagement and honest feedback from consortium members and piloting agents.

**Implementation details:**
- **Participants:** 26 DC4EU piloting agents and consortium members
- **Data collection method:** Real-time via menti.com during structured workshop session
- **Response quality:** High engagement with 60-80% response rate across all questions
- **Survey structure:** Four thematic evaluation blocks designed for comprehensive assessment

### Survey Structure and Assessment Areas

#### Block 1: Production Readiness Assessment
Evaluation of current readiness for real-world deployment of DC4EU solutions, including challenge identification and priority assessment.

#### Block 2: Internal eIDAS2/EUDIW Perceptions  
Analysis of institutional awareness, management buy-in, and internal dynamics regarding eIDAS2 implementation and investment requirements.

#### Block 3: Post-Project Commitment
Critical assessment of genuine willingness and capacity to continue digital credential work beyond the formal project conclusion.

#### Block 4: Strategic Vision
Evaluation of preferred future scenarios and architectural priorities for European digital credential ecosystem development.

## Strategic Survey Outcomes and Implications

### Production Readiness Assessment Results
The survey revealed a predominantly positive readiness sentiment (65% confident/prepared responses) whilst identifying specific deployment challenges:

**Primary challenge categories:**
1. **Regulatory/legal issues** (highest priority - 30.5% allocation)
2. **Resource constraints** (24.5% priority allocation)  
3. **Organisational resistance** (21.3% priority allocation)
4. **Technical obstacles** (lowest priority but still significant)

### Post-Project Sustainability Insights
Critical findings for continuation planning:
- **75% willingness** to continue beyond project conclusion
- **Funding dependency** as primary sustainability constraint
- **Institutional leadership commitment** required for successful transition
- **Scenario 2 preference** as practical starting point for implementation

### Strategic Vision Consensus
The survey established consensus around pragmatic implementation approach:
- **Scenario 2 implementation focus** as realistic foundation
- **Progressive evolution** towards more comprehensive scenarios
- **Community-driven development** model for ongoing collaboration
- **eIDAS2 preparation** as key strategic priority
