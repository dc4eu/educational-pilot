# DC4EU Warsaw Workshop Survey - "Beyond DC4EU" Assessment

## Introduction

This survey was conducted during the DC4EU Warsaw workshop by Work Package 9 (WP9) using a guided presentation format with menti.com for real-time response collection. The survey focused on assessing the current state of DC4EU solutions and evaluating aspects related to the future beyond the project's end on 31st July 2025.

The survey was structured around four critical evaluation blocks designed to capture honest assessments from piloting agents about production readiness, institutional dynamics, post-project commitment, and strategic vision.

---

## Survey Structure and Questions

### **Block 1: Production Readiness Assessment**

*"We want your honest assessment of where our current DC4EU solutions stand in terms of real-world deployment."*

#### Question 1: Initial Pulse Check
**Format:** Single word response for word cloud generation

**Question:** What is one word that describes your current readiness for the DC4EU solutions?

**Instructions:**
- Don't overthink this - what's your gut reaction?
- Could be: 'confident', 'uncertain', 'prepared', 'overwhelmed', 'optimistic', 'concerned'
- This isn't about technical specifications - it's about where you are emotionally and practically
- No wrong answers - if you're feeling 'confused', say that. If you're 'excited', that's equally valuable

---

#### Question 2: Specific Deployment Challenges
**Format:** Open text response

**Question:** What challenges do you foresee in moving DC4EU solutions to production?

**Context:**
Think about your real-world context: your institution's infrastructure, your team's capacity, regulatory requirements, funding constraints, technical integration needs.

**Examples of challenge types:**
- Technical issues like system integration
- Organisational challenges like getting management buy-in
- Regulatory hurdles like compliance requirements
- Resource constraints like staffing or budget
- External dependencies like Member State infrastructure that's not ready yet

**Instructions:** Be specific - instead of just 'technical challenges', what kind? Instead of 'funding', what specific funding gaps?

---

#### Question 3: Challenge Prioritisation
**Format:** Ranking exercise (1-4 priority order)

**Question:** Please prioritise the following implementation challenges you face:

**Challenge categories to rank:**
1. **Technical obstacles** - system integration, infrastructure gaps, interoperability issues, technical skills shortages
2. **Organisational resistance** - management hesitation, departmental silos, change management challenges, lack of institutional champions
3. **Lack of resources** - funding constraints, staffing limitations, time pressures, competing priorities
4. **Regulatory compliance** - uncertainty about eIDAS2 requirements, legal frameworks not being ready, gaps between current scenarios and what Member States can actually deliver

**Instructions:** Rank from 1 (biggest challenge) to 4 (least concerning) based on YOUR specific institutional situation.

---

### **Block 2: Internal eIDAS2/EUDIW Perceptions**

*"This examines how eIDAS2 and EUDIW are perceived within your institutions - not about technical readiness, but about organisational awareness, management buy-in, and internal dynamics."*

#### Question 4: Institutional Perception Assessment
**Format:** Open text response

**Question:** How is eIDAS2 perceived within your institution?

**Focus areas:**
- Internal conversations (or lack thereof) about eIDAS2
- Management reactions when it comes up in meetings
- Different stakeholder group perspectives (senior management vs IT teams vs legal department vs operational staff)
- Whether people see it as opportunity or regulatory burden
- Level of understanding vs. abstraction

**Instructions:** 
- Don't give the 'official line' from strategy documents
- Focus on reality of corridor conversations, meeting dynamics, and actual day-to-day sentiment
- Consider reactions: Excitement? Confusion? Indifference? Concern about compliance costs?

---

#### Question 5: Investment Requirements Assessment  
**Format:** Open text response

**Question:** What internal investments are necessary for eIDAS2 compliance?

**Investment categories to consider:**
- **Infrastructure** - new servers, security systems, backup facilities, integration platforms, cloud services
- **Personnel** - new hires, training, consultants, dedicated resources, skills gap filling
- **Compliance and legal** - legal advice, audit processes, certification procedures, ongoing monitoring
- **Integration work** - connecting eIDAS2 with existing systems, data migration, testing
- **Ongoing operational costs** - not just setup, but year-after-year maintenance

**Instructions:** 
- Be realistic about scale - thousands, tens of thousands, or hundreds of thousands?
- Consider timeframe for investment
- Focus on magnitude rather than precise figures

---

### **Block 3: Post-Project Commitment** 

*"Our most critical discussion - with DC4EU ending on 31st July, we need to understand your genuine willingness and capacity to continue this work."*

#### Question 6: Continuation Willingness and Motivation
**Format:** Open text response

**Question:** If the DC4EU project ends, would you want to continue the work? Why or why not?

**Considerations:**
- Institutional support availability
- Budget allocation reality
- Staff time constraints  
- Management backing
- Competing priorities
- Value perception in continuing

**Instructions:**
- Absolute honesty required - not what you think we want to hear
- If 'yes' - explain the value you see and what motivates continuation
- If 'no' or 'uncertain' - help us understand the barriers
- If conditional - specify what those conditions would be
- No shame in saying you can't continue - we need realistic assessments

---

#### Question 7: Critical Elements for Continuity
**Format:** Ranking exercise (1-4 priority order)

**Question:** Rank the critical elements necessary for post-project continuity:

**Elements to rank:**
1. **Funding stability** - predictable, adequate budget allocation for operations, staff, and development (internal funding, new European projects, or other revenue streams)
2. **Technical support** - ongoing technical expertise access (internal capacity, consortium collaboration, or external support for maintenance and development)
3. **Institutional commitment** - leadership genuinely backing this work with policy support, resource allocation, and strategic priority (active commitment, not just tolerance)
4. **Stakeholder engagement** - active participation from broader community (universities, professional bodies, government agencies, industry partners who validate and use services)

**Instructions:** Rank 1-4 based on what YOUR institution needs most to continue effectively.

---

#### Question 8: Preferred Future Role
**Format:** Multiple choice or open text

**Question:** What role would you prefer in future initiatives?

**Role options:**
- **Technical Leader** - driving architecture decisions, standards development, innovation workstreams (requires significant technical expertise and time commitment)
- **Piloting Agent** - testing, implementing, providing real-world feedback on solutions (similar to current role but perhaps more focused)
- **Strategic Advisor** - contributing expertise and guidance without day-to-day operational responsibilities (providing direction based on experience and sectoral knowledge)
- **Implementation Partner** - focusing on deployment, integration, and practical rollout activities within your domain or geography
- **Other** - specify alternative that leverages your specific expertise or institutional position

**Instructions:** Consider your realistic capacity and what level of engagement you can actually sustain while adding value.

---

### **Block 4: Strategic Vision**

*"Where do you see yourselves positioning within the eIDAS regulatory framework, and what architectural elements are non-negotiable?"*

#### Question 9: eIDAS Scenario Evolution Strategy
**Format:** Open text response

**Question:** What is your vision for the future of eIDAS scenarios?

**Current context:**
- Currently operating primarily in Scenarios 1 and 2 (closed systems and non-qualified Trust Service Providers)
- Regulatory framework points toward Scenarios 3 and 4 (public sector bodies issuing Pub_EAAs and qualified TSPs issuing QEAAs)
- Scenarios 3 and 4 offer stronger legal recognition and cross-border validity but require significant Member State infrastructure that largely doesn't exist yet

**Strategic positioning options:**
- Evolving toward authorised public sector bodies under Scenario 3 (strong legal standing but requires formal government recognition and strict regulatory compliance)
- Maintaining flexibility of non-qualified TSP model under Scenario 2 (limited automatic legal recognition but easier implementation and lower regulatory burden)
- Hybrid approach - starting with Scenario 2 but building toward Scenario 3 as Member State frameworks mature
- Alternative vision for evolution

**Instructions:** Consider both regulatory compliance and fundamental trust/business model implications for digital credentials in your sector.

## Expected Outcomes

This comprehensive survey was designed to provide:

1. **Realistic production readiness assessment** - honest evaluation of deployment barriers and institutional capacity
2. **Internal dynamics understanding** - insights into organisational perception and investment willingness  
3. **Post-project sustainability evaluation** - genuine commitment levels and required support structures
4. **Strategic direction consensus** - shared vision for eIDAS scenario evolution and architectural priorities

The results inform the transition from the formal DC4EU project conclusion to sustainable, community-driven continuation of digital credential initiatives across European educational and professional qualification sectors.
