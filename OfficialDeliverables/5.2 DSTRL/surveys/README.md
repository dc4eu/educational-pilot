# DC4EU Survey Analysis Directory

**Deployment and Testing Scenarios Results Library - Survey Component**

This directory provides comprehensive access to all DC4EU survey instruments, analysis, and results conducted throughout the project lifecycle. The surveys capture critical feedback from both institutional piloting agents and end-users across all three pilot implementations.

---

## Survey Organisation by Work Package

### Work Package 5 (WP5) Surveys

WP5 conducted systematic user experience monitoring through two complementary survey instruments targeting different participant groups within the educational ecosystem. Both surveys follow a common methodological framework designed to assess usability, trust, and implementation readiness across all pilot implementations.

#### Piloting Agents Survey
**Target Audience:** Educational institutions, VET providers, and qualification authorities implementing DC4EU solutions

**Context:** Institutional feedback survey designed to assess organisational readiness, technical implementation experience, and strategic positioning for post-project continuation. The survey captures insights from 23 educational institutions across 9 European countries, providing comprehensive institutional perspectives on deployment challenges and opportunities.

**Survey Instrument:** [Piloting Agents Survey Questions](./piloting-agents/piloting_agents_survey.md)

**Analysis and Results:**
- [Complete Analysis Overview](./piloting-agents/README.md)
- [Pilot 1 Analysis - Classical PKI](./piloting-agents/pilot1_comprehensive_analysis.md)
- [Pilot 2 Analysis - Decentralised PKI](./piloting-agents/pilot2_comprehensive_analysis.md)
- [Pilot 3 Analysis - Combined Implementation](./piloting-agents/pilot3_comprehensive_analysis.md)

#### End-User Survey
**Target Audience:** Students, learners, and educational professionals using wallet technology

**Context:** User experience assessment focusing on wallet usability, credential processes, and trust perceptions. This survey evaluates the practical user journey from credential issuance through verification, capturing feedback on interface design, process clarity, and system reliability from the end-user perspective.

**Survey Instrument:** [End-User Survey Questions](./end-users/end_users_survey.md)

**Analysis and Results:** [End-User Survey Analysis](./end-users/)

#### WP5 Survey Methodology
Both WP5 surveys employ a standardised methodological framework combining quantitative metrics with qualitative feedback mechanisms. The methodology emphasises systematic data collection across diverse educational contexts to enable comprehensive analysis of user experience, technical performance, and institutional readiness.

**Methodology Documentation:** [Comprehensive Survey Methodology](./survey_methodology.md)

### Work Package 9 (WP9) Survey

#### DC4EU Warsaw Workshop Consultation
**Target Audience:** DC4EU piloting agents and consortium members

**Context:** Strategic consultation survey conducted during the Warsaw workshop focused on gathering insights for post-project initiatives. The workshop survey addressed production readiness, institutional eIDAS2 perceptions, continuation commitment, and strategic vision for future digital credential implementations. Data collection was conducted real-time via menti.com during the workshop session.

**Survey Scope:** 26 participants providing feedback across four thematic blocks covering readiness assessment, institutional dynamics, post-project sustainability, and strategic direction.

**Survey Questions and Results:** [Warsaw Workshop Survey Analysis](./warsaw-workshop-survey.md)

## Survey Applications and Strategic Value

### Implementation Planning
The survey results provide evidence-based guidance for technical development priorities, institutional readiness assessment, and resource allocation decisions. Implementation teams can leverage institutional feedback to identify common challenges and successful deployment strategies.

### Policy and Standards Development
Survey findings inform eIDAS2 implementation pathways, standards adoption recommendations, and cross-border verification framework development. The comprehensive institutional feedback provides evidence for regulatory preparation and policy guidance.

### Quality Improvement
User experience feedback drives interface design improvements, process simplification, and training programme enhancement. Technical performance data guides system reliability improvements and integration capability development.
