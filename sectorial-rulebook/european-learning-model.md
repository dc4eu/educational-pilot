# DC4EU Standards Selection: ELM as the Optimal Payload Credentials Standard

## Executive Summary

This document presents the DC4EU project's justification for selecting the European Learning Model (ELM) as the standard for payload credentials in academic and professional qualifications. Following comprehensive evaluation of alternative standards, DC4EU has determined that ELM provides superior capabilities for cross-border contexts and complete learning path lifecycle support from primary through to adult education, making it the optimal choice for European digital credential infrastructure.

## DC4EU's Strategic Selection of ELM v3

### Alignment with Project Objectives

DC4EU's selection of ELM v3 reflects the project's commitment to establishing a robust, interoperable European digital credential ecosystem. The standard's comprehensive capabilities directly address the project's core requirements for academic and professional qualification management across diverse European educational contexts.

## ELM v3 Strengths Analysis: DC4EU's Selection Criteria

### Core Capabilities Meeting DC4EU Requirements

**Complete credential coverage** - ELM v3 demonstrates comprehensive handling of credentials across the entire educational spectrum, from formal qualifications (Bachelor's, Master's, Doctorates, VET certificates) to non-formal education (micro-credentials, adult education achievements) and informal learning (workshop participation, MOOC completions). This extensive coverage includes detailed achievement records, learning outcomes, assessment details, and issuer information, providing a unified approach to academic achievement documentation that spans all educational contexts.

**Detailed institutional information** - The standard incorporates extensive organisational details including legal institution names, accreditation status, contact information, digital identification, and quality assurance information. This comprehensive institutional representation ensures credential provenance and supports cross-border recognition processes whilst maintaining compliance with European frameworks.

**Structural flexibility** - ELM v3 strategically incorporates elements from multiple established standards including ELMO v2.1, OCCAPI v1, EWP Courses v0, and Edu-API v1, creating a cohesive framework. Built upon the W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model foundation, ELM extends this structure with detailed specifications for representing educational achievements, qualifications, and learning outcomes that meet the specific needs of European education systems through extensive collaboration between educational institutions, government bodies, and technical experts.

**Infrastructure management** - The standard addresses physical and digital infrastructure aspects including rooms and building details, learning delivery methods, and geographic context. This bridges the gap between digital credentials and physical educational infrastructure, supporting comprehensive facility management and spatial information integration.

**Academic relationships** - ELM v3 effectively manages complex associations including enrolment processes, student-course relationships, and other academic links. The standard supports cross-border agreements, framework alignments, and European Education Area mobility initiatives, facilitating seamless institutional coordination and student mobility across European borders.

## Comparative Analysis: Why DC4EU Rejected Alternative Standards

DC4EU's comprehensive evaluation process examined multiple existing standards, ultimately determining that alternative options failed to meet the project's requirements for comprehensive European digital credential infrastructure.

### ELMO v2.1 Limitations
- **Scope**: Limited primarily to basic credentials
- **Coverage**: Educational results/grades and accreditation/QA details
- **Limitation**: Narrow focus restricts broader educational ecosystem integration

### OCCAPI v1 Constraints
- **Focus**: Solely concentrated on organisational details and structure
- **Coverage**: Programmes and courses
- **Limitation**: Lacks comprehensive credential and assessment capabilities

### EWP Courses v0 Restrictions
- **Specialisation**: Very specific to programmes/courses and their instances
- **Limitation**: Insufficient for broader educational data exchange requirements

### Edu-API v1 Boundaries
- **Scope**: Limited to rooms/building details and basic associations
- **Coverage**: Enrolment processes only
- **Limitation**: Inadequate for comprehensive credential management

## DC4EU's Justification for ELM v3 Selection

DC4EU's selection of ELM v3 is based on the standard's demonstration of exceptional capability through six key characteristics that directly align with project objectives:

**Federating model functionality** - ELM v3 integrates and unifies the strongest characteristics of all other standards, creating a comprehensive framework rather than competing alternatives. The model's strength lies in its comprehensive approach to representing complex educational concepts including learning outcomes and achievements, credit systems (such as ECTS), assessment methods and grading schemes, professional competencies and skills, and quality assurance information.

**Holistic coverage provision** - The standard ensures comprehensive coverage across all educational contexts, supporting primary, secondary, tertiary, and adult education, as well as formal, non-formal, and informal learning achievements. ELM aligns with established European frameworks including the European Qualifications Framework, European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations (ESCO), Europass Digital Credentials Infrastructure, Bologna Process terminology, and Learning Opportunities and Qualifications in Europe.

**Interoperability enablement** - ELM v3 facilitates seamless integration with data from different sources and standards, promoting cross-institutional collaboration. The standard enables uniform structuring of credential data, allowing educational and professional qualifications to be recognised across all member states without additional modifications. This interoperability supports existing Bologna Process tools including ECTS credits, degree cycle indicators, qualification framework levels, and quality assurance status.

**Privacy and security assurance** - ELM v3 incorporates robust privacy-preserving features including selective disclosure capabilities, allowing users to share only necessary portions of their credentials whilst maintaining data integrity. The standard includes cryptographic proofs that safeguard personal data, ensuring credential holders can control what information is shared in line with GDPR principles. Personal identification remains compliant with privacy laws whilst supporting comprehensive educational profile linkage.

**Scalability assurance** - The framework adapts effectively to different educational contexts, from local institutions to international programmes. ELM's data model allows for encoding information relevant to different educational settings and levels, including qualification descriptions, learning outcomes, accreditation details, and the context of the learning process, whilst supporting customisation for different credentialing needs.

**Future-proof architecture** - ELM v3's structure accommodates evolution and extensions, ensuring long-term viability and adaptability. The model supports semantic consistency across implementations, enables extensions following established patterns, provides comprehensive multilingual support, and maintains explicit alignment with European frameworks whilst considering privacy requirements for holder information.

**European sovereignty alignment** - The standard ensures European educational data sovereignty, maintaining control over educational credentials within European frameworks, directly supporting DC4EU's mission to establish European digital credential independence. ELM supports EU strategies including the Micro-Credentials Framework, Digital Education Action Plan, and integrates with EBSI and eIDAS standards for enhanced verifiability and trust across European systems.

## DC4EU's Strategic Roadmap: ELM Evolution Opportunities

### DC4EU's Identified Enhancement Areas

DC4EU recognises that whilst ELM v3 currently provides the most comprehensive standard available, strategic evolution opportunities exist to achieve complete ecosystem coverage.
**From Edu-API v1:**
- Association management including enrolment processes and student-course relationships
- Comprehensive rooms/building details covering physical infrastructure and facilities

**From OOAPI v5:**
- Enhanced rooms/building details providing comprehensive facility management and spatial information

### DC4EU's Strategic Evolution Framework

**Infrastructure integration** - Incorporating comprehensive facility and space management capabilities would complete the physical-digital educational environment mapping.

**Relationship management** - Adding detailed association and enrolment processes would strengthen institutional coordination capabilities.

**Physical-digital mapping** - Connecting digital learning elements with physical spaces and resources would create a complete educational ecosystem representation.

### Strategic Advantages Supporting DC4EU's Selection

**Complete ecosystem coverage** - Evolution would eliminate gaps currently filled by fragmented standards, providing unified coverage.

**Total consolidation potential** - ELM v3 could become the definitive European standard for all educational data exchange, reducing complexity and improving efficiency.

**Enhanced institutional management** - Complete institutional operational coverage would streamline administrative processes and improve data consistency.

**Seamless integration capability** - The model's architecture supports incorporating additional elements without disrupting existing implementations, ensuring smooth transition paths.

## DC4EU's Conclusion: ELM as the Definitive European Standard

DC4EU's comprehensive evaluation concludes that ELM v3 represents the optimal choice for European digital credential infrastructure. Through incorporating identified enhancement opportunities, ELM v3 will achieve total comprehensiveness, establishing itself as the definitive European standard that DC4EU requires for successful project implementation.

The strategic positioning of ELM v3 as a federating model, combined with its inherent flexibility and comprehensive coverage, validates DC4EU's selection and provides the foundation for creating a unified European digital credential ecosystem. This selection eliminates the need for fragmented alternative standards whilst ensuring complete coverage of the educational ecosystem, from basic credentials to complex institutional relationships and physical infrastructure management.

DC4EU's commitment to ELM v3 represents a strategic investment in European educational sovereignty and interoperability, positioning the project to deliver comprehensive digital credential solutions that serve the entire European educational community.
