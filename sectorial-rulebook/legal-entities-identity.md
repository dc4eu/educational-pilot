# Legal Entity's Identity

## Overview

Educational institutions and professional qualifications bodies need reliable digital identification through public key infrastructure (PKI) with X.509v3 digital certificates. This system assigns unique digital identifiers to these entities, linking them with public certificates for seamless identity verification across European systems.

## The Opportunity: Harmonised European Legal Entity Identification

The European Digital Identity framework, through the integration of EBSI (European Blockchain Services Infrastructure) and standardised PKI mechanisms, creates an unprecedented opportunity for legal entities in the educational and professional qualifications sector to establish trusted, interoperable digital identities at the European level. This harmonised approach transforms how educational institutions and professional qualifications bodies operate and interact across member state boundaries.

## Key Components

### Public Key Infrastructure (PKI)
The PKI provides a trust framework that enables secure digital interactions between educational institutions and other entities in the ecosystem.

### X.509v3 Digital Certificates
These certificates:
- Provide cryptographic proof of institutional identity
- Contain standardised attributes for educational entities
- Support certificate validation and revocation
- Enable digital signing of credentials and documents

### EBSI Integration
The European Blockchain Services Infrastructure provides additional trust layers that complement the Classical PKI model:
- Decentralised identifier (DID) registration for institutions that can be bound to X.509v3 certificates
- Trusted issuer registry management with certificate-based verification
- Verifiable accreditation tracking linked to PKI credentials
- Cross-border institutional verification through certificate validation

**Important Note**: While EBSI provides enhanced capabilities, the fundamental trust model remains based on Classical PKI with X.509v3 certificates as mandated by eIDAS. EBSI serves as a complementary layer that enhances rather than replaces the certificate-based trust infrastructure.

## Implementation Considerations

When implementing legal entity identification for educational institutions and professional qualifications bodies under the eIDAS Classical PKI trust model:
- Certificate issuance processes must validate institutional legitimacy for both educational and professional entities according to eIDAS requirements
- Certificate attributes should align with European educational registries and professional qualifications frameworks whilst meeting X.509v3 standards
- Key management practices must follow eIDAS-compliant security best practices applicable to both sectors
- Certificate renewal and revocation processes must be clearly defined for all entity types and comply with Classical PKI requirements
- Technical integration should support existing institutional systems in both educational and professional contexts whilst ensuring X.509v3 certificate validation
- EBSI DID binding, where implemented, should complement rather than replace the underlying X.509v3 certificate infrastructure
- Staff training on new digital identity processes is essential for both educational and professional staff, with emphasis on Classical PKI certificate management
- Compliance auditing must verify adherence to eIDAS Classical PKI requirements for both issuer and relying party roles

## Integration with Trust Frameworks
