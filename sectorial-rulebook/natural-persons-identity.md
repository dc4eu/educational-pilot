# Natural Person's Identity

## Overview

The European Digital Identity wallet serves as a harmonised electronic identification method, introducing personal identification data (PID) as the preferred option when high security is needed. This system respects member state authority over identity while creating a consistent European approach.

## The Opportunity: A New Harmonised European Identification Method

The conjunction of the European Digital Identity Wallet (EUDIW) and the Personal Identification Data (PID) represents a historic opportunity to establish the first truly harmonised identification method at the European level. This innovation transcends national boundaries whilst respecting member state sovereignty, creating a unified digital identity infrastructure that serves both citizens and service providers across the European Union.

### Value Proposition for Citizens

This harmonised identification system offers unprecedented advantages for European citizens in cross-border contexts:

**Seamless Cross-Border Mobility**: Citizens can access educational and professional services across any EU member state using a single, trusted digital identity. Whether enrolling in a university abroad, applying for professional recognition, or accessing educational services, the same identification method works consistently across all 27 member states.

**Enhanced Privacy and Control**: Citizens maintain full control over their personal data, deciding what information to share with whom and when. The system implements privacy-by-design principles, ensuring minimal data disclosure and preventing unnecessary data collection by service providers.

**Reduced Administrative Burden**: No more need to navigate different national identification systems or repeatedly provide the same documentation. The "once-only" principle means citizens authenticate once and can reuse their verified identity across multiple services and jurisdictions.

**Legal Certainty and Recognition**: The harmonised system provides legal certainty that their digital identity will be recognised and accepted across the European Union, backed by the eIDAS 2.0 regulatory framework.

### Transformative Opportunities for Service Providers

The harmonised European identification method unlocks significant opportunities for service providers to innovate and improve their offerings:

**Universal European Market Access**: Service providers can now serve citizens from all EU member states with a single identification integration, dramatically expanding their potential market without the complexity of managing 27 different national identification systems.

**Enhanced Service Innovation**: The standardised identification method enables service providers to develop new digital services that work seamlessly across borders. Universities can offer pan-European programmes, professional bodies can provide EU-wide certification, and educational platforms can serve the entire European market with consistent user experiences.

**Operational Efficiency Gains**: Service providers benefit from:
- Reduced integration complexity (one system instead of 27 different national systems)
- Lower compliance costs through standardised regulatory requirements
- Streamlined customer onboarding processes
- Automated identity verification with high assurance levels
- Reduced fraud risk through cryptographically secured identity verification

**New Business Models**: The harmonised system enables entirely new business models:
- Pan-European educational consortiums with shared student identities
- Cross-border professional mobility services
- European-wide credential recognition platforms
- Integrated educational and professional development pathways

**Competitive Advantage**: Early adopters gain significant advantages by offering services that work seamlessly for all European citizens, whilst competitors remain limited to their national markets or struggle with complex multi-national integration requirements.

## Key Components

### Personal Identification Data (PID)
The PID provides a standardised set of identity attributes that can be reliably verified across member states while respecting national sovereignty in identity management.

### European Digital Identity Wallet
The wallet provides a secure container for identity credentials that can be used across the European educational ecosystem.

## Implementation in Educational Contexts

This approach streamlines identity verification in educational contexts, particularly in two main processes:

### Registration and Enrolment
When students begin their educational journey, secure identification is required to:
- Create educational accounts
- Verify eligibility for programmes
- Establish student records
- Link to national educational systems

### Formal Degree Issuance
When official qualifications are awarded, reliable identity confirmation is needed to:
- Ensure credentials are issued to the correct individual
- Create tamper-proof links between credentials and identity
- Support cross-border verification of qualifications
- Maintain the integrity of educational systems

## Integration with Existing Systems

Integration with existing educational identity systems requires:
- Mapping between institutional identifiers and European Digital Identity
- Privacy-preserving linking protocols
- Consent management for data sharing
- Clear audit trails for identity verification events

## Cross-Border Considerations

For cross-border educational mobility, the system provides:
- Recognition of identification across member states
- Consistent identity verification processes
- Maintenance of identity sovereignty
- Support for multilingual identity attributes

## Strategic Impact

The harmonised European identification method represents more than a technical advancement—it is a foundational building block for the European Education Area and the Digital Single Market. By providing a trusted, interoperable, and privacy-preserving identification infrastructure, it enables the realisation of European integration goals whilst maintaining the highest standards of security and citizen rights protection.

This system positions Europe as a global leader in digital identity innovation, providing a model that balances technological advancement with fundamental rights protection, and demonstrating how supranational cooperation can enhance rather than diminish national sovereignty in the digital age.
