# Annex C — Stakeholders and empirical evidence

## C.1 Purpose of the annex

This annex consolidates the map of stakeholders who participate in the W3C-VCDM ecosystem applied to lifelong learning, together with the empirical evidence that underpins the affirmations of chapters 01–10. The objective is to offer an external reviewer a navigable index that verifies who does what and with which backing.

## C.2 European institutions

### C.2.1 European Commission

- **DG CONNECT** — unit responsible for eIDAS 2.0, EUDIW and the Implementing Acts of Regulation 2024/1183.
- **DG EMPL** — Europass Team, responsible for the European Learning Model (ELM v3.2) and the Europass Dataspace.
- **DG EAC** — Directorate-General for Education, Youth, Sport and Culture.
- **DG GROW** — directorate-general with responsibilities over regulated professional qualifications (Directive 2005/36/EC).
- **Publications Office of the EU** — custodian of the controlled vocabularies under `http://data.europa.eu/snb/`.

### C.2.2 European Parliament

- **Committee on Industry, Research and Energy (ITRE)** — competent on digital identity and standardisation.
- **Committee on Culture and Education (CULT)** — competent on cross-border educational recognition.
- **Resolution P10_TA(2026)0022** on European digital sovereignty.

### C.2.3 Council of the EU

- **Council Recommendation of 16 June 2022** on microcredentials for lifelong learning and employability.

## C.3 Standardisation bodies

- **W3C Verifiable Credentials Working Group** — develops VCDM, VC-Data-Integrity, VC-JOSE-COSE, Bitstring Status List.
- **W3C Data Integrity Community Group** — develops the cryptosuites (ECDSA, BBS).
- **W3C Credentials Community Group** — discussion forum with active European participation.
- **ETSI TC ESI** — Electronic Signatures and Infrastructures, responsible for ETSI TS 119 472-1, ETSI TS 119 472-3, **ETSI TS 119 182-1 (JAdES)** and ETSI TS 119 312 (cryptographic algorithms, under revision to include privacy-preserving mechanisms).
- **OpenID Foundation** — OID4VCI, OID4VP, HAIP.
- **IETF OAuth Working Group** — SD-JWT, SD-JWT VC.
- **IRTF Crypto Forum Research Group (CFRG)** — BBS signatures (draft-irtf-cfrg-bbs-signatures); underpins the `bbs-2023` cryptosuite.
- **ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 17** — mdoc (ISO/IEC 18013-5).
- **UNESCO** — ISCED-F classification.

## C.4 Large Scale Pilot projects (DEP)

### C.4.1 DC4EU — Digital Credentials for Europe

- **Funding**: Grant Agreement 101102718, €19M Digital Europe Programme.
- **Coordinator**: University of Murcia (ES).
- **Coverage**: 80 organisations in 23 Member States.
- **Educational scope**: 36 institutions in 16 Member States, 89 % of the EU population.
- **Credentials issued**: 2,790 real, 18 types of the Sectoral EAA Catalogue.
- **Cryptographic profile**: JAdES D-Zero, QSealC, QWAC, QTS, ES256/EdDSA (operative today); BBS+ (forward-compatible extension).
- **Relevant deliverables**: D5.1.1 (Justification), D5.1.2 (Catalogue), D5.2 (Pilot report).
- **Validated wallet implementations**: Identify (Izertis), UAegean, Netcompany, Cappatrust.

### C.4.2 Other convergent European LSPs

- **EBSI-VECTOR** — educational and professional mobility over EBSI.
- **TRACE4EU** — traceability and mobility credentials.
- **PH4H — Pharmaceuticals for Health** — sectoral credentials in health.
- **Safe Island** — credentials in the social sphere.
- **Catena-X / Gaia-X** — industrial data spaces with Verifiable Credentials.

### C.8.2 Global W3C-VC wallet and verifier implementations

The interoperability map at **https://canivc.com** documents W3C-VC conformance across more than 50 global wallet and verifier implementations. This evidence confirms that European W3C-VC credentials — including those in the EUDIW profile — are verifiable by the global ecosystem without reissuance, and that the format is the convergent global standard for digital credentials.

Selected implementations with European relevance:

- Digital Bazaar (USA, W3C VC reference).
- MATTR (New Zealand/Finland, member of the European ecosystem).
- Procivis (Switzerland).
- Sphereon (Netherlands).
- Walt.id (Austria).
- Animo Solutions (Netherlands).
