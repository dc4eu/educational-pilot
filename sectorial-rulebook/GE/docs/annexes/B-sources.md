# Annex B — Sources

## B.1 Inclusion criteria

The sources listed in this annex satisfy at least one of the following criteria:

1. They are Recommendations published by recognised standardisation bodies (W3C, ETSI, ISO, IETF, OpenID Foundation).
2. They are normative texts in force or normative proposals published by the European Commission, the Council or the Parliament.
3. They are official deliverables of projects funded by the European Commission with a traceable project number.
4. They are publications of consortia or associations representative of the sector whose technical authority is recognised (EBSI, DG EMPL, DG CONNECT, CRUE, EUNIS, 1EdTech, Credential Engine, Groningen Declaration Network).

Citations are grouped by family: EU normative texts, W3C Recommendations, ETSI specifications, OpenID and IETF specifications, ISO standards, DEP/EEA deliverables, institutional literature.

## B.12 Note on source traceability

All the cited W3C Recommendations are publicly accessible at https://www.w3.org/TR/ without paywall, with stable canonical URIs. ETSI specifications are public and downloadable from https://www.etsi.org/standards. EU texts are accessible at https://eur-lex.europa.eu/. DC4EU deliverables are accessible on the project portal and, after the pilot's completion, will be transferred to the successor institutional repository designated by the Commission.
